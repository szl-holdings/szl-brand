Our discipline

Make the boundary part of the work.

Keep claims scoped.

Evidence and operational state travel separately. MEASURED, REPORTED, ROADMAP and UNAVAILABLE labels stay visible.

Keep the source close.

Follow a claim to its repository, observation and declared limitations. A public demo does not establish production authorization.

Keep uncertainty explicit.
